Our Mission & Core Values

Independent Nursing Advocacy Built on Human Connection

At The Advocate RN, our mission is to ensure no patient, senior, or family caregiver in Michigan ever feels rushed, unheard, or confused by the healthcare system. Because we work independently for you—not an insurance company or hospital network—every conversation is guided by our five core pillars:

01

Transparency

Honest, plain-language explanations of your care options, test results, and upfront pricing with zero hidden fees or agendas.

02

Trust & Integrity

Rooted in the ethical standards of the nursing profession—protecting your privacy, autonomy, and best interests at every step.

03

Respect

Honoring your personal values, cultural background, and family goals so your care plan aligns with what matters most to you.

04

Rapport

Creating a calm, judgment-free space where you can ask any question without ever feeling rushed by a 15-minute appointment clock.

05

Relationships

Walking alongside patients, adult children, and caregivers while fostering collaborative partnerships with your medical team.

Michigan RN Scope of Practice Assurance

Practiced in full accordance with the Michigan Public Health Code (Act 368) under an active Michigan Registered Nurse license.

Within RN Advocacy Scope: Health literacy education, explaining lab/imaging reports, medication reconciliation review, discharge planning, and specialist visit preparation.
Medical Boundary: We do not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe or change medications, order lab tests, or replace your licensed physician.
